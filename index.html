Part 1

Early Foundations & Naming

1838 – 1994 · The pioneers who gave us the language of immersive technology

1838
Stereoscope Invented
Sir Charles Wheatstone — binocular vision concept; ancestor of VR depth perception
1962
Sensorama
Morton Heilig — first VR-like machine with 3D display, sound, scent, and fans
1968
The Sword of Damocles
Ivan Sutherland & Bob Sproull (MIT) — first head-mounted display with head tracking
1974
Videoplace
Myron Krueger — interactive artificial reality lab; forerunner of AR interaction
1987
Term: 'Virtual Reality'
Jaron Lanier (VPL Research) — officially coined the term 'virtual reality'
1992
Term: 'Augmented Reality'
Tom Caudell & David Mizell (Boeing) — coined the term in a research paper
1994
Reality–Virtuality Continuum
Paul Milgram & Fumio Kishino — defined the spectrum from real to fully virtual
Sources: Marr (2021) · HQ Software Lab (2026) · TechCrunch (2023) · VR Expert (2022)
Part 2

The Modern Era

1998 – 2020s · When XR became consumer reality

1998
AR in Sports Broadcasting
Sportvision — first yellow yard marker overlaid on a live NFL broadcast
2010
Oculus Rift Prototype
Palmer Luckey (age 18) — reignited global interest in VR; Kickstarter raised $2.4M
2014
Facebook & Google Enter XR
Facebook acquires Oculus for ~$2B; Google launches Cardboard and Glass AR glasses
2015
AR Goes Mainstream
Snapchat launches face filter Lenses; millions experience AR daily on smartphones
2016
Microsoft HoloLens
First major Mixed Reality headset — holographic computing anchored to real space
2016
Pokémon GO
Niantic — AR game reaches 500M+ downloads; largest mass AR event in history
2020s
XR Across Industries
VR/AR/MR adopted in healthcare, education, manufacturing, law enforcement, and more
Sources: Marr (2021) · Capsule Sight (2023) · Verizon (2022) · HQ Software Lab (2026)
